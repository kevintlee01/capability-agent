How this works

  1. 1Gemini opens a real Chromium window against the mock bank app and reads the live accessibility tree.
  2. 2It decides one action at a time — click, fill, navigate — until the goal is reached or it gets stuck.
  3. 3On success, the whole flow is saved as a typed, versioned capability artifact.
  4. 4From then on, Replay re-runs that artifact deterministically — no LLM, no re-reasoning.
Heads up

A real browser window will pop up on your screen for ~10-15 seconds while Gemini works. Don't close it — it closes itself when the run finishes.
